Your iPhone.
At home in your car.

JSPLAY is a CarPlay port custom-built for French cars (Peugeot/Citroen) with Hangsheng 'big blue screen' head units. The hardest part - the Bluetooth handshake - took nearly two months to solve, and steering-wheel controls for the Citroen C5 X on Hangsheng hardware are already integrated. Audio routing and CarPlay video streaming are still being refined; TCP currently runs on port 7000. Community contributions toward a smoother port are very welcome.

Build from source ↓How to install

No jailbreak, dongle, account or authentication server required.

Install on the car, not the iPhone. Wireless uses the built-in car hotspot or Wi-Fi Direct (Android 10+). Compatibility depends on the head unit, firmware and iOS version.

Purpose-built for French cars (Peugeot/Citroen) with Hangsheng big-blue-screen head units, especially the Citroen C5 X. Other vehicles are untested and unsupported.

Install on your car

  1. Build the app from source using the build guide, then install the APK using the method supported by your head unit.
  2. Open JSPLAY and allow the requested Bluetooth, Wi-Fi/location and microphone permissions.
  3. Turn on the car’s built-in hotspot (5 GHz if available). In Settings → Connection setup, select Built-in car hotspot and save its exact name and password. Keep phone Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, pair with the car, then tap Connect phone and accept the phone prompts.
  4. For wired use, connect a USB data cable and choose Connect with USB. Approve any USB, Trust or local VPN prompts.

ADB is not required for everyday use. APK installation options depend on the car. No manual MAC address entry.

In this release

  • CarPlay song metadata and album artwork for compatible Android launchers and media displays.
  • AirPlay port fallback when a factory listener occupies the preferred port; Wi-Fi Direct handles unknown security reports and busy channels.
  • Targeted USBMUX padding recovery preserves split and combined frames; USB startup accepts unfinished Manual hotspot settings.
  • Call echo cancellation and noise suppression through supported Android effects, depending on the head unit.
  • BYD battery reporting selects CAN or CANFD through existing network ADB; available readings depend on vehicle firmware.
  • Show or hide the home-screen map mirror while keeping the instrument-cluster map; more Ukrainian translations for map and widget controls.
  • Optional video with seeking and ten-second skips: only in P with network ADB and valid gear data. Protected FairPlay video remains unsupported.
  • Bounded Bluetooth, USB, reconnect, boot and microphone diagnostics help investigate failures without recording audio or packet contents.
Build guide ↗

Before you try it

A public preview, not an Apple-certified product. Authentication uses an experimental accessory identity supplied by the local release process; future acceptance is not guaranteed. Some head units still show stuttering or ignore icon-size changes. Start with 30 fps and HEVC off; try a lower resolution if needed.
